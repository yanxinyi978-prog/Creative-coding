Music-Reactive Bouncing Balls

Explore the relationship between sound, movement and colour. Play the music, watch the circles react, and click the canvas to change their behaviour.

Music is paused. Press Play Music to begin.

How to interact

  • Press Play Music to start the music.
  • Watch the circles change size with the music volume.
  • Click inside the animation to change colours and speeds.
  • Press Pause Music to pause the sound.

Made with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and p5.js.
